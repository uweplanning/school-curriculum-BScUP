This interactive visualisation allows you to explore how the subjects you study at school links on to the BSc Urban Planning at UWE Bristol, and what further career pathways it leads you on to.

The curriculum on the BSc Urban Planning programme (https://courses.uwe.ac.uk/K401/urban-planning) is guided by the following PLOs (Programme Learning Outcomes) – similar to Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in Secondary Schools.

View the 10 Programme Learning Outcomes (PLOs)
PLO 1 Understand Planning (Pathway 1 – Planning for healthy environments) - Explain land use planning processes and enabling sustainable development using core planning competencies as well as drawing on relevant planning theory.
PLO 2 Global and Local Change (Pathway 2 – Biodiversity and energy management) - Recognise and respond to local and global challenges, demonstrating critical thinking.
PLO 3 Systems thinking (Pathway 3 – Planning for connected urban/rural communities) - Demonstrate the ability to apply systems thinking at different spatial scales extending to both urban and rural areas.
PLO 4 Inequality and Positive Change (Pathway 4 – Urban regeneration and masterplanning) - Define, analyse and address economic, social, political, environmental and health inequalities.
PLO 5 Risk Management (Pathway 5 – Protecting our historic places) - Evaluate environmental and economic risks, awareness of viability and the need to balance different agendas.
PLO 6 Legal and Institutional Frameworks (Pathway 6 – Project management) - Explain land use planning in various legal contexts and analyse economic, social, political, environmental and health data effectively.
PLO 7 Community Engagement (Pathway 7 – Engaged communities, changing places) - Evaluate community engagement's importance and respond to technological changes in interdisciplinary environments.
PLO 8 Evidence and Research (Pathway 8 – Smart cities and open data) - Judge evidence quality, use digital tools and modelling techniques to find solutions for complex problems.
PLO 9 Design and Aesthetics (Pathway 9 – Designing high quality places) - Apply design principles to create high-quality places, considering long-term impacts on people and the planet.
PLO 10 Professional Competencies and Standards (Pathway 10 – Reflective practitioner) - Demonstrate communication, negotiation, and leadership skills whilst upholding ethical behaviour.
